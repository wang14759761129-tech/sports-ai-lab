# Analysis plan

**Status:** PLANNED / standard-library preparation

## Level 1 — Data quality

Check required column names, missing or unknown values, invalid categories, duplicate point IDs, and score consistency.

## Level 2 — Descriptive

Summarise serve and receive distributions, third-ball attack rate, point win rate, and average rally length. Keep denominators visible.

## Level 3 — Comparison

Compare short and long serves, broad serve locations, and spin categories only when counts are sufficient and definitions are stable.

## Level 4 — Statistics

Confidence intervals, effect sizes, association tests, and regression are **NOT YET IMPLEMENTED**. They require stronger foundations and a justified dataset.

## Level 5 — Modeling

Machine learning is FUTURE. It is not a substitute for a clear question, valid labels, or an honest baseline.
