# Inter-rater reliability

**Status:** FUTURE METHOD — not completed.

Two people with relevant table tennis understanding should independently annotate the same video segment using the same versioned guide. Compare agreement for serve location, serve length, receive type, and third-ball attack. Disagreements should be reviewed without silently rewriting the original labels.

The purpose is to test whether the variables are defined clearly enough that results do not depend only on one observer's judgement. A future analysis may report a suitable agreement measure after sample size and category balance are understood; no reliability statistic is claimed now.
