# Limitations

- Small sample during the pilot stages.
- Single-player or limited-match context.
- Manual annotation and possible observer bias.
- Serve spin may be uncertain from video.
- Outcomes depend on opponent, score, tactics, and context.
- Video selection can create selection bias.
- The observational design does not support causal inference.

The value of this project is to practice careful data and research methods, not to make an early large claim.
