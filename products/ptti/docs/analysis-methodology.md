# Evidence and interpretation

Reports describe recorded points, not unrecorded play or player ability. An annotation may be subjective; both teammates should audit sample points and agree on the protocol. Missing optional annotations reduce each metric's denominator; they are not losses or zeros.

The deterministic engine emits FACT statements for service, receive and third-ball conversion with exact counts. Fewer than 20 opportunities are labeled small sample; 20+ remains descriptive only, not a statistical significance claim. A frequent-attack / poor-conversion interpretation requires at least 10 annotated service points, 60%+ attack rate and below 50% conversion. Its evidence is included, and it suggests point review without claiming cause. No confidence intervals, significance tests, opponent adjustment or fabricated coaching advice.

The export contains structured evidence with player, kind, metric and reliability. Future LLM reports must preserve this fact/interpretation distinction and cite actual points. Current UI metric links provide broad server filtering, not exact numerator/denominator drilldown; the explorer exposes all original fields.
