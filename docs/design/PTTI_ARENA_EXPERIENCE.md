# Arena / The Competition Lounge

Implemented PROTOTYPE: static 9:9 game-five scenario → choose A/B → lock/reveal → append history → next independent round. Results: odd rounds B, even rounds A. The rule is visible; no AI prediction or win probability is claimed. Every round begins from the same scenario, not a simulated advancing match.

Layout: scoreboard/court left, choice panel right, history below. Selection has aria-pressed and a border; reveal disabled without a choice; settled round immutable; next round clears selection. Stored history resumes at the following round on reload. No money, rewards, chips, odds or live indicators.
